## About

I build digital systems for businesses — not just websites. Through **AxentriX**, I design and develop client portals, business dashboards, and full-stack platforms for real estate firms, retail brands, and service companies across Lebanon and the Gulf.

My work spans the full stack: from brand identity and UI design to production-grade Next.js applications with authentication, real-time data, and admin systems.

<br>

## What I Work On

- **Web Applications** — Next.js, Firebase (Auth + Firestore), Vercel deployments
- **Client Portals & Dashboards** — custom systems for managing projects, orders, and clients
- **E-Commerce Platforms** — full storefronts with cart, inventory, and order tracking
- **Brand Identity** — logo design, visual systems, typography, and guidelines
